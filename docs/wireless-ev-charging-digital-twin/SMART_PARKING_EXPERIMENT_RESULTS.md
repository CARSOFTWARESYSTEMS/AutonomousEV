## Configuration

- **Replications:** 50 per policy and scenario. Seeds are `20261127 + 101·k` for k = 0…49. Every policy sees the same seeds, so arrivals, vehicles and patience are identical (common random numbers); comparisons are paired.
- **Facility:** 12 bays (8 conventional including 1 accessible, 4 wireless), the default layout. The day runs 07:00–19:00 with a 5 s step.
- **Equal resources for every policy:**
  - the same bays, and any vehicle may use any bay (the accessible bay needs a permit);
  - an 11 kW battery-side charger in each wireless bay, with grid-to-battery efficiency 0.937;
  - the same canopy PV, site import limit, 1.2 kW base load and IN tariff;
  - a compatible EV that asked to charge charges whenever it is in a working wireless bay.
- **Queue:** 4 places at the entry. Patience is exponential with a 8-minute mean.
- **Manual baseline (a declared assumption, not an observation):** drivers take the first free bay they pass, and 25 % miss and loop the aisle. Search time is the drive at 70 % of aisle speed plus 6 s.
- **Peak import:** the largest 15-minute average grid import.
- **Fairness:** Jain's index of per-request energy fulfilment (delivered / requested). Requests that got no wireless bay count as 0.
- **Fault recovery:** queued vehicle-minutes above the same seed's fault-free day, and the minutes after the fault until the queue matches that day for 10 consecutive minutes.
- **Notation:** values are means with their 95 % confidence interval [low, high] (Student t). Differences are paired against the manual baseline. *n.s.* means the interval includes zero.

## Policies

- **Manual parking (baseline)** — No guidance: drivers take the first free bay they pass (any type, the accessible bay only with a permit); one pass in four misses and loops the aisle.
- **First available bay** — Guided to the lowest-numbered free bay (N01…N06, then S01…S06), any type.
- **Nearest available bay** — Guided to the free bay with the shortest driving distance from the entry, any type.
- **Charging-compatible allocation** — Compatible EVs that asked to charge go to the nearest working wireless bay; every other vehicle to the nearest conventional bay, and to a wireless bay only when no conventional bay is free.
- **SOC-aware allocation** — As charging-compatible, but the last free wireless bay is kept for a vehicle whose energy need (from its SOC and target) is at least 8 kWh.
- **Energy-aware allocation** — Charging-compatible allocation plus charging power scheduled by least laxity under a grid import target of 15 kW above the PV available; a session that would otherwise miss its target by departure gets full power.

## Limitations

- **No vehicle motion or aisle congestion.** Search time does not delay the entry queue, so waiting time depends only on bay occupancy. The facility simulation (SP-SIM-001) models motion, and its runs are not part of these tables.
- **Fixed charging power.** Charging is a constant 11 kW battery-side, with no CC–CV taper. Check SP-A09 bounds the error of this surrogate against the Station model at 3.5 % for a 30→80 % session.
- **Synthetic inputs.** Demand, stays, the SOC on arrival, patience and driver behaviour are declared distributions, not data.
- **No storage in the dispatch.** The research model has no battery storage; PV not used at the moment it is generated is counted as unused.
- **Tariff.** Revenue uses the configured IN tariff, a representative placeholder (SMART_PARKING_INTERNATIONALIZATION.md). It predicts no behavioural response to price.
